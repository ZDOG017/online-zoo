Western lowland gorillas are the smallest of the four gorilla subspecies. They live in thick tropical rainforests, where they spend much of their day foraging for fruits, leaves, shoots, nuts, and other plants, as well as insects and small invertebrates. Their powerful arms, broad chests, and strong jaws help them navigate and thrive in dense forest habitats. Western lowland gorillas live in family groups led by a dominant silverback, who protects the troop and mediates conflicts. Sadly, habitat destruction, hunting, and disease have caused serious declines in wild populations. By observing Glen and his family, we can better understand their behavior and continue working toward their protection.
